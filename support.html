Common questions

  • I pressed play and nothing happens. The music starts with your first running steps: start running. To hear music without running, tap the tempo button, choose Fixed tempo, then press play.
  • The first song takes a while. It is downloaded and measured before it plays, about 15 seconds. The next songs are prepared in the background.
  • No music, and "Can't reach Audius". The songs stream over your data connection. The app keeps trying and continues as soon as the connection is back.
  • Where should I carry the phone? Anywhere: a pocket, your hand, an armband or a belt, with the screen locked.
  • Will the songs sound strange? No. The speed changes by at most 10% and the pitch is kept.
  • Does it use a lot of data? About 10 MB for a four-minute song, like any music streaming app.
  • Can I use Spotify or my own music? Not at the moment: streaming services don't let other apps change their songs' speed.
  • Does it track my runs? No. It counts steps per minute only to set the tempo, and stores no history.
  • Who made a song? Tap the song's cover: its back shows the artist, the licence and a link to the song on Audius.
