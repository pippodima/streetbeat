Music that runs at your pace

StreetBeat listens to your steps and gently speeds up or slows down each song, so its beat matches your stride. The pitch stays natural. Press play, put the phone in your pocket, and run.

The music follows you

Running to a matching beat feels easier: your feet fall into the rhythm and the kilometres pass more quickly. But no playlist can follow you when you speed up on a hill or slow down at the end. StreetBeat turns it around. It doesn't ask you to run at the music's pace. The music follows yours.

Your steps set the tempo

The phone counts your steps per minute. Songs close to that tempo are adjusted by up to 10%, too little to hear, so the beat lands on your feet. Speed up or slow down, and the music follows within seconds.

Continuous, beat-aligned mixes

Each song fades into the next exactly on the beat, so your feet never have to find the rhythm again. Long intros without a clear beat are skipped.

It notices stops and walks

At a traffic light the music lowers, then pauses, and returns when you run again. On a walk break it keeps your tempo, a little quieter. No need to touch the phone.

Soft sound cues

Short, quiet bell sounds tell you what the app noticed: ready, on your beat, speeding up, walk break. Rising means going, falling means easing off.

Anywhere you carry it

No watch, chest strap or armband needed. A pocket, your hand, an armband or a belt all work, with the screen locked. Play, pause and skip from your headphones or the lock screen.

Music by independent artists

The big streaming services don't let other apps change the speed of their songs, so StreetBeat plays music from Audius, an open platform where independent artists publish their work. There are hundreds of songs at any running tempo in most genres; choose the genres you like. Tap a song's cover to see its credits and open it on Audius.

Nothing about you leaves the phone

No account, no ads, no analytics, no run tracking. Your steps set the tempo and are never stored. Read the privacy policy.
