StreetBeat privacy policy

Effective 19 October 2026.

In short

StreetBeat has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Nothing about you is sent to its developer.

What stays on your phone

  • Your steps. While the app is open, it reads the phone's motion sensor to count your steps per minute. The readings set the music's tempo at once; they are not saved and not sent anywhere.
  • Your settings: the genres, sounds and other choices you make, and the tempo of your last run. They are saved on the phone only, and deleting the app deletes them.
  • Songs. Each song is downloaded to the phone to be played, and deleted after it has played. Anything left is deleted the next time the app starts.

What goes to Audius

The music comes from Audius (audius.co), an open music platform. To find and play songs, the app asks Audius's servers for songs at your tempo and in the genres you chose, then downloads the songs and their covers. Like any website, Audius's servers see your phone's internet (IP) address with these requests. StreetBeat sends no name, account or other identifier of yours. How Audius handles this is described in the Audius Privacy Policy: https://audius.co/documents/PrivacyPolicy.pdf

Links you open

"Open on Audius" and the links on the About page open in your browser. From then on, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Apple and Google

The App Store and Google Play may collect information about downloads and crashes under their own privacy policies. StreetBeat's developer sees only the statistics and crash reports they share with every developer, which do not identify you.

Children

StreetBeat is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date.

Contact

Write to the contact address on StreetBeat's page in the App Store or Google Play.
